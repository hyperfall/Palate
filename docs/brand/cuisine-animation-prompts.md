# Cuisine card animations — Seedance 2.0 prompts

One 5-second, perfectly-looping animated scene per open kitchen, replacing the
static cuisine card images. Each animation visualises the exact editorial line
already printed on its card, so the motion and the copy read as one idea.

## Global settings (apply to every generation)

- **Duration:** 5 seconds. **Aspect:** 4:3 (the card crop is 800×600 — if the
  tool insists on 16:9, keep all action in the centre 4:3 safe zone).
- **Perfect loop:** first and last frame must be identical. Every motion in the
  scene is cyclical (steam, sway, stirring, flicker) with a period that divides
  5 seconds evenly. **Camera locked — no pans, no zooms** (camera motion is the
  #1 loop killer).
- **Style, every clip:** hand-drawn 2D animation, cartoon shapes with rich
  painted detail, warm textured flat colours with soft paper grain — like a
  living cookbook illustration. Cozy, golden, appetising light. Food is always
  vivid and full-colour, never dimmed or desaturated.
- **Palette anchors (the brand):** warm cream ground `#F1F2EC`, deep pan green
  `#26301F` for shadows/night, flame orange `#E4572E` as the accent that
  appears once per scene (a flame, a lantern, an ember).
- **Avoid, every clip:** text, lettering, signage words, logos, flags,
  photorealism, camera movement, cuts, humans in photoreal style, dim or
  muted food.

A reusable style block to prepend to each prompt below:

> Hand-drawn 2D cartoon animation with rich painted detail, warm textured flat
> colours and subtle paper grain, cozy golden light, cookbook-illustration
> style. Static locked camera, no cuts. Seamless 5-second loop: first and last
> frame identical, all motion cyclical. No text, no logos, no photorealism.

## Chinese — "Heat here has a texture, not just a temperature."

**Scene.** A night-market wok station seen straight on. A cartoon cook in a
white tee tosses a huge carbon-steel wok over a roaring burner; glossy noodles
and scarlet chillies leap in an arc. Two red paper lanterns hang above; steam
and one curl of wok-flame lick the rim. Background: deep green night with warm
window glows.

**The loop.** The wok toss is the metronome: ingredients rise and fall in a
2.5-second cycle, twice per clip. Flame flickers and lantern sway share the
same period. Cook's stance never changes.

**Prompt.**
> [style block] A street wok station at night: a cartoon cook rhythmically
> tosses a large wok over a fierce orange flame, glossy noodles and red
> chillies arcing up and landing in perfect cycles, a ribbon of flame licking
> the wok rim on each toss, steam pumping upward in repeating puffs, two red
> lanterns swaying gently overhead, deep forest-green night behind with warm
> window lights. The toss repeats exactly twice, ending mid-air exactly as it
> began.

## Indian — "Spices bloomed in fat until the kitchen changes its mind about what food smells like."

**Scene.** Overhead-slight-angle view of a brass tadka pan tilted over a bowl
of golden dal on a cream ground with a marigold at the corner. Mustard seeds,
curry leaves and red chilli slide from the pan in a glittering stream; the dal
surface blooms with orange-red swirls of tempered oil.

**The loop.** The pour is continuous and never empties — a steady sparkling
stream. The bloom on the dal swirls in a closed circle; seeds pop in a
repeating 1.25-second sparkle rhythm; steam wisps cycle.

**Prompt.**
> [style block] A small brass tadka pan pours an endless sparkling stream of
> mustard seeds, curry leaves and sizzling golden oil into a bowl of creamy
> yellow dal; where it lands, flame-orange oil blooms and swirls in a closed
> circle; tiny seeds pop with star-like sparkles in a steady rhythm; gentle
> steam rises in repeating wisps; one marigold and scattered whole spices rest
> on a warm cream cloth. The pour never starts or ends — a perfect loop.

## Italian — "Few ingredients, ruthlessly chosen. The discipline is in what gets left out."

**Scene.** A sunlit marble counter, three-quarter view. A hand-cranked pasta
machine feeds out one continuous golden sheet that folds into soft ribbons.
Beside it, exactly three things, spotlit like museum pieces: one tomato, one
basil sprig, one bottle of olive oil. A linen curtain breathes at a window.

**The loop.** The crank turns at a constant rate; the pasta sheet is an
endless belt (its fold pattern repeats every 2.5 seconds). Curtain sway and a
drifting dust-mote share the cycle. Nothing else moves — the restraint IS the
animation.

**Prompt.**
> [style block] A sunlit marble counter: a hand-cranked pasta machine turns
> steadily as an endless silky golden pasta sheet emerges and folds into neat
> ribbons, the fold pattern repeating in a perfect cycle; beside it sit only a
> single ripe tomato, one basil sprig, and a small bottle of green-gold olive
> oil, still and proud; a soft linen curtain breathes slowly at a bright
> window. Calm, warm, precise. The crank and folds end exactly where they
> began.

## Japanese — "Restraint as a technique. Dashi, soy, and mirin in careful proportion — nothing to hide behind."

**Scene.** A quiet counter in side view: one earthen pot of dashi barely
trembling over a low flame, a single ladle resting across it. Behind, a navy
noren curtain with no lettering sways at a doorway; a maple branch drops a
single leaf that dissolves into steam-mist and re-forms.

**The loop.** Three synchronized cycles: the dashi's surface shivers; one
steam thread rises and curls; the noren breathes. The falling leaf completes
one fall per 5 seconds, fading out at the pot and reappearing at the branch —
the loop's hidden seam.

**Prompt.**
> [style block] A serene wooden counter: a small earthen pot of clear golden
> broth trembles gently over a tiny blue-orange flame, one thin thread of
> steam rising and curling in a repeating pattern; a plain indigo noren
> curtain sways softly in a doorway behind; a single red maple leaf drifts
> down, dissolves into the steam, and reappears at the branch above.
> Minimal, precise, warm. Every motion cycles; the final frame matches the
> first.

## Korean — "Fermentation doing the heavy lifting. Kimchi, gochujang, doenjang — time is the first ingredient."

**Scene.** A hanok rooftop terrace at golden hour: a family of dark onggi
(clay fermentation jars) of different sizes. The nearest jar's lid tips ajar;
scarlet kimchi peeks out and tiny fermentation bubbles rise and pop above it
like slow champagne. A stone bowl of stew steams beside the jars.

**The loop.** Bubbles rise-pop on a steady cycle; the steam column billows in
a repeating shape; a jar lid rocks almost imperceptibly with the ferment's
breathing. Light doesn't change.

**Prompt.**
> [style block] A rooftop terrace with a family of round dark clay
> fermentation jars glowing in golden-hour light; the nearest lid sits ajar
> with brilliant red kimchi peeking out, small bubbles rising and popping
> above it in a slow steady rhythm, the lid rocking a millimetre as if the jar
> is breathing; beside it a stone bowl of red stew pumps soft steam in
> repeating billows; tiled hanok roofs in deep green shadow behind. The
> bubbles and steam cycle perfectly back to the opening frame.

## Levantine — "Lemon, olive oil, herbs by the handful. Food designed to sit on a table for hours."

**Scene.** Overhead view of a white bowl of swirled labneh at the centre of a
patterned tablecloth crowded at the edges with mezze plates. A glass jug pours
a slim ribbon of green-gold olive oil into the labneh's swirl; za'atar and
torn herbs drift down like confetti. Dappled light through a vine.

**The loop.** The oil ribbon pours endlessly, tracing the swirl in a closed
circle that refills itself. Herb-fall repeats; the vine-leaf shadows shimmer
on a cycle. Plates never move — the table is patient.

**Prompt.**
> [style block] Overhead: a white bowl of thick swirled labneh at the centre
> of a busy mezze table; a slim endless ribbon of green-gold olive oil pours
> from above, tracing the swirl in a closed glistening circle that never
> overflows; pinches of dark za'atar and bright herb leaves drift down gently
> like confetti in a repeating fall; dappled vine-leaf shadows shimmer across
> the patterned tablecloth. Abundant, unhurried, warm. The pour and shadows
> cycle seamlessly.

## Mexican — "Chillies toasted until they smell of chocolate and smoke. Patience is an ingredient."

**Scene.** A clay comal over embers, three-quarter view. Dark ancho and
guajillo chillies toast and puff gently; two ropes of blue-grey smoke rise and
braid themselves. Above, a string of papel picado (cut-paper banners, no
lettering — geometric holes) flutters. A molcajete waits at the side, and one
ember pulses flame-orange.

**The loop.** Smoke braids in a repeating figure; chillies puff-settle on a
2.5-second cycle; papel picado ripples with the heat; the ember brightens and
dims like a heartbeat.

**Prompt.**
> [style block] A clay comal rests over glowing embers: dark red dried
> chillies toast and gently puff in a slow repeating rhythm, two ropes of
> blue-grey smoke rising and braiding into a repeating pattern; a string of
> colourful cut-paper banners with geometric patterns flutters above in the
> heat shimmer; a volcanic stone mortar waits nearby; one ember pulses
> flame-orange like a heartbeat. Smoky, patient, warm. Every motion returns
> exactly to the opening frame.

## Thai — "Built on the balance of four things at once — hot, sour, salty, sweet. Nothing dominates for long."

**Scene.** A wooden market table, straight on: a clay mortar at centre where a
pestle pounds som tam in rhythm. Orbiting the mortar in a slow circle, like
planets in balance: a red chilli (hot), a halved lime (sour), a small fish
sauce bottle (salty), a knob of palm sugar (sweet). Behind, a floating-market
canal glitters.

**The loop.** The pestle pounds four beats in 5 seconds; the four ingredients
complete exactly one full orbit; the canal glitter twinkles on the same
period. Four flavours, four beats, one circle — balance, animated.

**Prompt.**
> [style block] A wooden market table: a clay mortar and pestle pound green
> papaya salad in a steady four-beat rhythm, tiny flecks jumping with each
> beat; around the mortar, four items orbit slowly in a perfect circle like
> planets — a red chilli, a halved bright lime, a small amber bottle, a knob
> of golden palm sugar — completing exactly one revolution; behind, a canal
> glitters with repeating sparkles under warm light. Balanced, rhythmic,
> playful. The orbit and pounding land precisely on the opening frame.

## Production notes

- **Loop QA:** scrub frame 1 vs frame 150 (at 30fps) — they must match
  pixel-close. If Seedance drifts, generate 6–7s and trim to the cleanest
  5-second cycle boundary in Picsart.
- **Export:** MP4 (H.264) + WebM, muted, ≤2MB each if possible — these
  autoplay on a grid of eight.
- **The flame accent:** each scene contains exactly one flame-orange element
  (burner, tadka oil, ember, lantern). That's the brand thread across all
  eight — resist adding more.
- **When the assets are ready**, the cuisine cards need a small code change to
  render a muted looping `<video>` with the current image as poster/fallback —
  ask and it's wired in.
